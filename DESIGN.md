## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, warm corporate SaaS aesthetic tailored specifically for high-conversion landing experiences targeting growing businesses and SMEs. It fuses the airy, luminous ambient gradient backdrops and generous breathing room seen in modern software showcases with an authoritative, grounded corporate wine/burgundy palette.

The visual tone radiates stability, modern clarity, and effortless efficiency:
- **Personality:** Trustworthy, polished, energetic, and accessible. It rejects cold, overly clinical fintech blues in favor of a warm, confident burgundy spectrum balanced by pure whites and soft rose-tinted neutral surfaces.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Light Minimal with subtle ambient mesh gradients and frosted glass elements. Layered cards, pill badges, and refined micro-shadows give depth without visual clutter, directing focus immediately toward conversion actions and product demonstrations.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-contrast, trustworthy hierarchy:
- **Primary Burgundy (`#8B1F3C`):** The foundational brand color representing governance, financial authority, and premium software craftsmanship. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key headline accents, and brand touchpoints.
- **Secondary Vibrant Burgundy (`#B03A5B`):** An energetic, lighter shade that brings warmth and conversion pull to badges, interactive hover states, gradient stops, and secondary accents.
- **Tertiary Rose Wash (`#FBEAEF`):** A delicate, low-saturation rose tint used for tag backgrounds, active container pills, and icon container surfaces.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E2229`):** A deep charcoal slate rather than pitch black, preventing harsh visual vibration against light backgrounds while maintaining crisp readability.

### Ambient Gradients & Canvas
- Hero and highlighted sections leverage gentle, non-distracting atmospheric radial or diagonal linear gradients blending `#FFFFFF`, `#F8F9FA`, and soft touches of `#FFF5F7` or `#FBEAEF` (5%–12% opacity) to evoke the airy, polished aesthetic of modern SaaS benchmarks.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric warmth and approachable corporate punch of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and action labels with the clinical legibility and neutral clarity of Inter for body text, data points, and descriptive copy.

- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and authoritative weights (600, 700). Important key terms in hero headers can be highlighted using the primary or secondary burgundy tones.
- **Body & Paragraphs (Inter):** Set with comfortable line-height multipliers (1.5–1.6) to guarantee immediate scanning for business owners and operational teams evaluating software capabilities.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Crisp, slightly tracked labels provide structure across SaaS metrics, navigation links, and badge capsules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid contained within a maximum width of `1240px` for landing pages, ensuring content remains dense, focused, and legible on ultra-wide screens.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` outer page margins. Section vertical spacing ranges from `5rem` to `7.5rem` (`80px` to `120px`) to achieve the open, breathable atmosphere of modern SaaS showcases.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Two-column feature grids stack or adjust into balanced 1-column layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` margins. Form groups, hero CTAs, and comparison cards reflow to full width with uniform vertical stack gaps of `space-md` to `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent surfaces, whisper-soft neutral-burgundy shadows, and low-contrast perimeter strokes:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and pale `#F8F9FA` foundational surfaces with optional gradient light bleeds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle border (`1px solid #EAECEF`) and ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards / Floating SaaS Mockups):** Elevated showcase elements, feature cards on hover, and dropdown menus: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(139, 31, 60, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.03)`, bordered by `#F2D6DF`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Navigation Bar):** Frosted glass effect with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `rgba(234, 236, 239, 0.8)`, and an ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature (`roundedness: 2`):
- Standard cards, interactive blocks, and form inputs utilize `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering a soft, non-intimidating, and tactile feel.
- App previews, product mockup shells, and featured pricing tiers utilize `1.5rem` (`24px`) (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-badges, notification pills, and interactive call-to-action buttons use full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to maintain energy, visual softness, and clear affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled gradient or solid primary burgundy (`#8B1F3C` to `#A12446`), text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-lg`, font weight `600`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Subtle lift on hover with `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(139, 31, 60, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** Border `1.5px solid #8B1F3C`, text `#8B1F3C`, background transparent. On hover: background `#FBEAEF`.
- **Ghost Button:** Text `#1E2229`, background transparent. On hover: background `#F8F9FA`, text `#8B1F3C`.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Announcement Chips:** Pill container (`rounded-full`) with background `#FBEAEF`, text `#8B1F3C`, small font size (`12px` / `13px`), weight `600`, and horizontal padding of `0.75rem`. Often paired with a small solid interior dot or arrow icon to link product news.

### Cards & Feature Tiles
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: `1px solid #EAECEF`.
- Radius: `1rem` (`16px`).
- Padding: `2rem` (`32px`).
- Feature icons inside cards are housed in `48x48px` rounded squares (`rounded-lg`) filled with `#FBEAEF` and rendered with `#8B1F3C` vector strokes.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`.
- Border: `1px solid #EAECEF`, radius `0.75rem` (`12px`), padding `0.875rem 1rem`.
- Focus State: Border color `#8B1F3C`, with an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 31, 60, 0.12)`.
- Labels are crisp, positioned above the field in `#1E2229` (weight `600`), and helper text is displayed in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20x20px`, corner radius `6px` for checkboxes and circular for radios.
- Checked State: Solid `#8B1F3C` fill with a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or inner circle. Unchecked border is `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.

### SaaS Metric & Trust Badges
- Lightweight counter blocks with tabular numerical typography, bold primary numbers, and small muted uppercase labels beneath, accompanied by soft monochrome customer logos displaying `50%` opacity to maintain unobtrusive social proof.
